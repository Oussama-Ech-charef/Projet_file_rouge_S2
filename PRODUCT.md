# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
PHP and JavaScript with JSON-file storage; stack selected from the explicit project brief.

## Users
Cabinet staff who organize patient records, consultations, specialties, and prescriptions.

## Product Purpose
A local web application for maintaining the four linked records used by a medical practice. Success means staff can view, add, edit, and delete records through a clear dashboard.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Consultations belong to a patient and a specialty; each consultation has at most one prescription. Data is stored as JSON files through a PHP API, with no SQL database. Consultation modes are in-person or teleconsultation; statuses are planned, completed, or cancelled.

## Brand Commitments
The brief specifies a dark side navigation, light gray workspace, white panels, and orange accents. The interface is in French.

## Evidence on Hand
No prototype or production data is provided. Any starter records are labeled as examples.

## Product Principles
- Make record lists easy to scan.
- Keep relationships visible and valid.
- Give clear feedback after each change.
- Preserve usability on narrow screens.
